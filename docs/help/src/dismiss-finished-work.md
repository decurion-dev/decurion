# Dismiss finished work

This page helps you clear finished work and goals off your screens without losing anything.

Work that is **Done** or **Failed**, and goals that are finished or stopped, stay in Decurion's lists until you dismiss them. Dismissing only hides them. The work, its branch, its pull request and its history all stay, and you can bring anything back with one tap.

## Where finished work shows

Home keeps itself tidy. **Done today** shows only what finished today, so done work leaves it on its own the next day. Failed work waits under **Needs you** until you retry it or dismiss it.

## Dismiss one piece of work

- Tap the work to open it, then tap **Dismiss** at the bottom of its panel.
- Failed work under **Needs you** also has its own **Dismiss** button, on your PC and on your phone, so it stops waiting on you.

## See all finished work, and bring it back

Every finished and dismissed piece of work is one tap away, with or without **Pro view**.

1. On Home, open **Done today**.
2. At the end of the list, tap **See all finished work**. It shows the **Done** and **Failed** lists, not just today's. **‹ Home** takes you back.

In **Pro view**, Home's **By status** shows the same lists among the others (on your PC, Done starts folded: click it; on your phone, tap **Done** or **Failed** at the top).

There you can:

- tap **Dismiss** on any card under **Done** or **Failed** (on your phone, open the card first);
- tap **Dismiss all** to clear every card in that column, for the app you are looking at;
- open **Dismissed** at the bottom to see what you dismissed, then tap **Bring back** on any of it.

## Goals

- **Dismiss all** next to **Finished** clears finished goals from the list.
- Open a finished goal and tap **Dismiss** at the bottom to clear just that one.
- Dismissed goals are under **Dismissed** at the bottom of the list. Open one and tap **Bring back**.

## Good to know

- Right after you dismiss something, the message at the bottom of the screen has an **Undo** button.
- Only finished things can be dismissed. Work that is running, waiting or needs you always stays on screen.
- If you retry failed work you dismissed, it comes back on its own.
- When you dismiss the work behind an app's last merged pull request, that app's line on Home stops repeating "merged" and goes quiet.
- The Roadmap already has its own **Hide finished** switch. See [Your apps and their Roadmaps](follow-your-roadmap.md).
