# Watch and take over the test browser

While Claude works on an app, it can open the app in a test browser to check its work. This page shows how to watch that browser live, and how to take it over to click and type in it yourself, on your PC or your phone.

## Watch the browser

1. On the board, click the card for work that is **Running** with Claude.
2. Under **Test browser**, click **Watch the browser**.

A live picture of the browser appears, with the address of the page above it. The address comes from the browser itself, so a page cannot pretend to be somewhere else.

If the picture does not show up after a few seconds, the agent may not have opened the browser yet. It starts the first time the agent uses it.

On a PC, click **Bigger** to fill the window. Click **Smaller** to go back.

## Take over

Taking over is useful when the agent is stuck on a step that is easier for you to do, like closing a pop-up or getting past a screen it does not understand.

1. Click **Take over**. The frame turns blue, and the agent is told to leave the browser alone until you hand it back.
2. Use the browser:

| To do this | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Click something | Tap or click it in the picture. |
| Scroll | Drag the picture up or down. On a PC, you can also use the mouse wheel. |
| Type | Tap the field in the picture first, then type in the box under the picture and tap **Send**. On a PC, you can also click the picture and type straight away. |
| Press a key | Tap **Enter**, **Tab** or **Backspace**. |
| Go back a page | Tap **Back**. |

3. When you are done, click **Hand back**. The agent is told it can use the browser again, and it looks at the page fresh before it carries on.

You don't have to remember to hand it back. The browser goes back to the agent by itself when you close the work panel, close the page, lose your connection, or don't touch it for 10 minutes.

The agent's activity log shows each time you took over or handed back.

## Keep your passwords safe

Don't type real passwords while you have taken over. The test browser belongs to the agent's run, and what you type there could be seen by the agent.

To keep an app signed in to a website for its tests, use the sign-in window instead. It is a separate browser that the agent never sees, and Decurion saves only the sign-in, never your password. Even then, use test accounts, not your own.

## What Decurion keeps

Nothing from the browser is saved. The picture and what you type pass straight through Decurion to the browser and are never written down. Decurion only keeps a record that you watched, and when you took over and handed back.
