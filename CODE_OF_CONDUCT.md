# Code of Conduct

Decurion follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Please read the full text there. It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and anywhere else you represent the project.

## The short version

- Be kind and respectful. Welcome people of every background, identity and level of experience.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and own your mistakes when you get something wrong.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, personal attacks, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people who use Decurion.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behavior worries you, please tell the maintainers privately. Use the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/decurion-dev/decurion/security) and choose **Report a vulnerability**; this opens a private report that only the maintainers can see. Start the title with "Code of Conduct" so we know it is not a security bug. We do not use an email address for reports.

All reports are handled in confidence. The maintainers will review each report and respond in a way they judge fair and appropriate, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant (correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban).
