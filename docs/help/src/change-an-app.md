# Change an app's plan, name or folder

This page helps you change how an app is billed, which Max plan it uses, its name or its folder, and which app goes first today.

## Change how an app is billed

1. Open **Settings**, then **Apps**, then the app.
2. Open the **Plan and money** tab.
3. Click **Switch to pay-per-use**, or **Switch to Max plan A** (or **B**) to go back.

The change applies to work that starts or resumes after you click.

## Change which Max plan it uses

1. On the app's **Plan and money** tab, choose **Max plan A** or **Max plan B** under **Claude Max plan**.
2. Click **Save plan**.

New work uses the new plan. Work already running keeps the plan it started with. Decurion never changes an app's plan by itself.

## Give an app its own cap

An app can have its own monthly cap on pay-per-use money, across Claude, ChatGPT and Grok.

1. On the app's **Plan and money** tab, type an amount in dollars in **Own monthly cap**.
2. Click **Save cap**.

To take the cap off, click **Remove cap**. The app then stops only at the caps in **Settings**, **Accounts**.

## Change an app's name or folder

1. Open **Settings**, **Apps**, then the app.
2. Open the **Name and folder** tab.
3. Change its **Name** or its **Folder**. Type the folder, or click **Choose folder**.
4. Click **Save**.

While some of the app's work is running, or paused partway, Decurion keeps the old name and folder, because that work keeps its files under them. Wait until the work finishes (or stop it), then change them. You can change the plan, the reviewer and the rules at any time.

## Remove an app

On the same **Name and folder** tab, **Remove app** hides the app from Decurion. See [Remove an app](add-your-first-app.md#remove-an-app).

## Let one app go first today

When one app matters most today, open **Settings**, **Apps**, then the app's **Plan and money** tab, and click **Go first today**.

Its waiting work then starts before other apps' work, and its line on Home says **First today**. It ends by itself at the end of the day. To end it sooner, click **Going first today**. It changes only the order: the money caps and plan limits still apply.

## Apps take turns

When several apps have work waiting, they take turns. After one app's work starts, the next turn goes to the app that has waited longest. One big goal does not hold up your other apps. Within one app, the oldest work goes first.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Another app is already called portal."** Pick a different name.
- **"Wait until this app's running work finishes, then rename it."** (or "then change its folder") Some of its work is running or paused partway. Let it finish, or stop it, then try again.
- **"This app isn't here any more."** The app's page was opened from an old link. Tap **Back to Apps** and pick it from the list.
