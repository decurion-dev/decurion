# Follow your master plan

This page helps you see, in one place, what each app has done and what is
left.

Each app has one **master plan**: a list of its goals, in your order, with
everything they found along the way. Open **Master plan** at the top of the
Goals page, or go to <http://decurion.localhost:7420/plan>. On your phone, tap
**Plan** at the bottom. Choose the app at the top right, or at the top on a
phone.

## What the plan shows

At the top you see how many things are done and how many are left, with a bar
that fills as work finishes. Turn on **Hide finished** to see only what is
left.

Each goal is a card. Its mark and words tell you where it is:

- **✓ Done** in green: you closed the goal.
- **◐ Working**: agents are building or checking it. Nothing for you to do.
- **! Needs you** in amber: the goal waits for you. The words after it say
  what, such as **Needs you · approve the plan**. Tap its name to open it.
- **✕ Stopped** in grey: you stopped the goal.

Under the name, the goal's steps show in order, each with a mark: ✓ done,
◐ an agent is on it now, ! **Needs you** (in amber), ○ not started yet. A
step marked **Failed** in red went wrong; tap it to open that piece of work
on the board.

The pages agents drew for the goal show as small buttons, such as
**Visual plan** or **As built: Order form**. One marked **Needs you · a
question** has a question for you.

Use the small arrows on the right of a card to move it up or down. The order
is yours; it does not change what agents do.

## What the project lead found

When work finishes, your project lead may notice more things worth doing: a
small bug, a missing detail, a good next step. These show under the goal as
**Found**, with a count of what is fixed and what is left. Each one says
**Found by your project lead**. Nothing happens to them until you tap.

- **Tick the box** when it is done, or no longer matters. Tap again to untick.
- **Fix this** starts a new goal to fix just that one. You go straight to it,
  and your project lead talks it through with you as usual.
- **Fix all that are left** starts one goal for every open one under that goal.
- **✕** takes it off the plan. Turn off **Hide finished** and tap **Put back**
  to bring it back.

When a goal started this way is done, the things it fixed are ticked for you.
Each one links to the goal fixing it: **Being fixed in** while it runs,
**Fixed in** once it is done.

You can add your own: tap **Add a to-do here** inside a goal, or use **Add to
the plan** at the bottom for something on its own. A to-do of your own has a
**Make it a goal** button when you are ready to have it built.

## The Pages tab

**Pages** lists every page agents drew for the app, newest first, grouped by
goal: **Visual plan** pages from before the work, and **As built** pictures
taken by the checker. Pages with a question for you are marked **Needs you ·
answer a question** (or how many). Tap **Needs you** to see only those.

## Answer questions beside a page

Tap any page to open it. The page fills the screen, and your answers sit
beside it. The page itself can't run anything or reach your PC; the buttons
around it are Decurion's own.

- **The agent asks**: tap a choice for each question. **Suggested** marks the
  agent's pick. Tap **Answer in your own words** to type instead, or as well.
  Some questions only take words. Then press **Send answers**. Your project
  lead gets them, and the page shows what you sent.
- **Ready to start?**: on the newest plan, press **Approve this plan** to start
  the work, or **Ask for changes** to say what should be different.
- **Your project lead asks**: questions from the conversation show here too,
  so you can answer without leaving the page.
- **How the check went**: on an **As built** page, the checker's verdict and
  what it noticed.
- **Tell your project lead something**: a message about the page, at any time.

When your answers or changes lead to a new plan, you don't need to go
anywhere. The page dims while your project lead works, says **Drawing the new
plan…** while the new picture is made, and then the new plan takes its place
by itself. **See the earlier one** opens the plan it replaced. If you open an
older plan on purpose, it stays put and offers **Show the latest plan**.

On a PC the answers sit on the right. On a phone, a bar at the bottom says how
many questions wait for you. Tap **Answer** to open them over the lower part of
the page, and **✕** to close them. **Back** takes you to where you came from;
**Open on its own** opens just the page in a new tab.
