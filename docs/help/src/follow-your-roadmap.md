# Your apps and their Roadmaps

This page helps you see, in one place, what each app has done and what is
left.

## Open an app

**Apps** is one of the four places in Decurion's menu: **Home**, **Goals**,
**Apps** and **Settings**. On your PC it is in the menu on the left; on your
phone it is a tab at the bottom. Or go to
<http://decurion.localhost:7420/plan>.

Apps opens on the list of your apps. Each row shows the app's name, how many
of its goals are in progress, and an amber **Needs you** count when something
in that app waits for you. Tap an app to open its page.

Once you pick an app, Decurion remembers it on that PC or phone: the next
time you tap **Apps** it opens that app straight away. Picking one app on
Home does the same. To go back to the list, tap **Apps** in the line at the
top (**Apps › pv-medispa**) on your PC, or **‹ Apps** on your phone. On a PC
you can also pick another app from the box beside its name.

Under the app's name is its line from Home: where its last pull request is,
with its **Run it**, **Merge**, **Bring up to date** or **Deploy** button when
it has one.

Each app's page has four tabs:

- **Roadmap**: every goal for the app, in your order, with everything they
  found along the way. It opens first.
- **Board**: only this app's work, one row per goal, with its pieces under
  **Waiting on you**, **Building**, **Checking** and **Done**. Tap a piece to
  open it.
- **Pages**: every page agents drew for the app. See [The Pages
  tab](#the-pages-tab).
- **Files**: briefs, specs and pictures agents read while they work on this
  app. See [Attach files for agents](attach-files.md).

**Rules and settings**, beside the app's name, opens the app's rules, its
plan and money, its websites and its name and folder in **Settings**.

The word **plan** on its own always means one goal's plan: the pieces of
work your project lead proposes and you approve. The Roadmap is the list of
all of an app's goals.

## What the Roadmap shows

At the top you see how many things are done and how many are left, with a bar
that fills as work finishes. Turn on **Hide finished** to see only what is
left.

Each goal is a card. Its words tell you where it is, and they are the same
words Home, the Goals page, the Board and your phone show for that goal:

- **Done**, **Live** or **On your PC** in green: the goal is finished.
- **Talking it through** or **Working**: your project lead or the agents are
  on it. Nothing for you to do.
- **Needs you** in amber: the goal waits for you. The words after it say
  what, such as **Needs you · approve the plan** or **Needs you · merge on
  GitHub**. Tap its name to open it.
- **Stopped** in grey: you stopped the goal.

Under the name, the goal's road shows its stages in order: **Ask**, **Plan**,
**Build**, **Check**, **Merge**, and **Live** (or **On your PC** for
Decurion itself) when the app has a step after the merge. See [See where a
goal is](give-your-first-goal.md#see-where-a-goal-is). Each piece of work
also has its own small button; tap it to open that piece. A
piece marked **Failed** in red went wrong.

The pages agents drew for the goal show as small buttons, such as
**Visual plan** or **As built: Order form**. One marked **Needs you · a
question** has a question for you.

Use the small arrows on the right of a card to move it up or down. The order
is yours; it does not change what agents do.

## What the project lead found

When work finishes, your project lead may notice more things worth doing: a
small bug, a missing detail, a good next step. These show under the goal as
**Found**, with a count of what is fixed and what is left. Each one says
**Found by your project lead**. Nothing happens to them until you tap.

- **Tick the box** when it is done, or no longer matters. Tap again to untick.
- **Fix this** starts a new goal to fix just that one. You go straight to it,
  and your project lead talks it through with you as usual.
- **Fix all that are left** starts one goal for every open one under that goal.
- The **trash can** deletes it. See [Delete and bring back](#delete-and-bring-back).
  A to-do taken off the Roadmap earlier shows **Put back**. Turn off **Hide
  finished** to see it.

When a goal started this way is done, the things it fixed are ticked for you.
Each one links to the goal fixing it: **Being fixed in** while it runs,
**Fixed in** once it is done.

You can add your own: tap **Add a to-do here** inside a goal, or use **Add to
the Roadmap** at the bottom for something on its own. A to-do of your own has a
**Make it a goal** button when you are ready to have it built.

## Delete and bring back

You can delete a to-do of any kind, and a goal that is **Done** or
**Stopped**. Tap the **trash can**, then **Delete** when it asks **Are you
sure?**. A goal that is still going has no trash can: stop it or finish it
first.

Deleting a goal only takes it off the Roadmap. Its work, its history and its pages
stay on the Goals screen and in Pages. The to-dos under it go with it.

Deleted things wait in the **Deleted** tab, newest first. Tap **Bring back**
and the row returns to the place it had. Bringing back a goal also brings back
the to-dos that went with it. A to-do you deleted on its own earlier stays
there until you bring it back too. If its goal is deleted, bring the goal back
first.

Tap **Empty the Deleted list** to remove everything there for good. It asks
**Are you sure?** first, and it cannot be undone. The work and its history
stay. Deleted things do not count in **done** or **left**.

## The Pages tab

**Pages** lists every page agents drew for the app, newest first, grouped by
goal: **Visual plan** pages from before the work, and **As built** pictures
taken by the checker. Pages with a question for you are marked **Needs you ·
answer a question** (or how many). Tap **Needs you** to see only those.

## Answer questions beside a page

Tap any page to open it. The page fills the screen, and your answers sit
beside it. The page itself can't run anything or reach your PC; the buttons
around it are Decurion's own.

- **The agent asks**: tap a choice for each question. **Suggested** marks the
  agent's pick. Tap **Answer in your own words** to type instead, or as well.
  Some questions only take words. Then press **Send answers**. Your project
  lead gets them, and the page shows what you sent.
- **Ready to start?**: on the newest plan, press **Approve this plan** to start
  the work, or **Ask for changes** to say what should be different.
- **Your project lead asks**: questions from the conversation show here too,
  so you can answer without leaving the page.
- **How the check went**: on an **As built** page, the checker's verdict and
  what it noticed.
- **Tell your project lead something**: a message about the page, at any time.

When your answers or changes lead to a new plan, you don't need to go
anywhere. The page dims while your project lead works, says **Drawing the new
plan…** while the new picture is made, and then the new plan takes its place
by itself. **See the earlier one** opens the plan it replaced. If you open an
older plan on purpose, it stays put and offers **Show the latest plan**.

On a PC the answers sit on the right. On a phone, a bar at the bottom says how
many questions wait for you. Tap **Answer** to open them over the lower part of
the page, and **✕** to close them. **Back** takes you to where you came from (the app's **Pages** tab when you
opened the page from there);
**Open on its own** opens just the page in a new tab.
