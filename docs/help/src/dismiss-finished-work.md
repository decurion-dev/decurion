# Dismiss finished work

This page helps you clear finished work and goals off your screens without losing anything.

Work that is **Done** or **Failed**, and goals that are finished or stopped, stay where you can see them until you dismiss them. Dismissing only hides them. The work, its branch, its pull request and its history all stay, and you can bring anything back with one tap.

## On the board (PC)

- Each card under **Done** or **Failed** has a **Dismiss** button.
- **Dismiss all** at the top of the column clears every card in it, for the app you are looking at.
- Dismissed cards fold away under **Dismissed** at the bottom of the column. Click it to see them, then **Bring back** on any card.

## On your phone

- **Inbox:** failed work you don't want to retry has a **Dismiss** button, so it stops waiting on you.
- **Board tab:** pick **Done** or **Failed**, then **Dismiss all**, or open a card and tap **Dismiss**. Dismissed cards are under **Dismissed** at the bottom of the list.

## Goals

- **Dismiss all** next to **Finished** clears finished goals from the list.
- Open a finished goal and tap **Dismiss** at the bottom to clear just that one.
- Dismissed goals are under **Dismissed** at the bottom of the list. Open one and tap **Bring back**.

## Good to know

- Right after you dismiss something, the message at the bottom of the screen has an **Undo** button.
- Only finished things can be dismissed. Work that is running, waiting or needs you always stays on screen.
- If you retry failed work you dismissed, it comes back on its own.
- When you dismiss the work behind an app's last merged pull request, that app's line in **Today** stops repeating "merged" and goes quiet.
- The master plan already has its own **Hide finished** switch. See [Follow your master plan](follow-your-master-plan.md).
