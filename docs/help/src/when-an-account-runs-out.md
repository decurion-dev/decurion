# When an account runs out

This page helps you keep work going when a Claude account or your pay-per-use money runs out.

## When pay-per-use money runs out

Decurion watches what pay-per-use work spends each month. The total shows at the top of the board, next to your cap.

When the month's total reaches your cap:

1. Pay-per-use work stops before it starts a new run and moves to **Needs you**.
2. Your phone gets a "Spending cap reached" alert.
3. The total at the top of the board shows that the cap is used up. It turns amber once you pass 80% of the cap.
4. Work on your Max accounts keeps going.

To continue:

1. On the board, open **Spending**.
2. Type a higher monthly cap and click **Save**.
3. In the [Anthropic Console](https://console.anthropic.com/), raise the workspace spend limit to the same amount.
4. Open each waiting card and tap **Continue**.

Or wait. The total starts again at zero on the first day of each month (in UTC time).

A single run can also reach its own limit. The agent then says "Stopped at this run's spending limit." Tap **Continue** to give it more of this month's budget.

## When a Claude Max account runs out

> **Coming soon.** This part of Decurion is still being built. This page shows how it will work.
>
> Decurion will show how much of each account's week you have used. When an account runs out, its work will pause, and an inbox card will ask what you want to do, such as continue on pay-per-use or wait until the weekly reset. Decurion never switches accounts on its own.

What you can do today:

- **Wait** for your Claude plan to reset, then click **Retry** on the failed work.
- **Move the app to pay-per-use.** On the board, click **Add an app**, then **Switch to pay-per-use** next to the app. Then click **Retry** on the failed work. See [Connect your Claude accounts](connect-claude-accounts.md) to set up the key first.
