# Talk to Decurion and hear it read aloud

This page shows how to speak instead of typing, answer an agent's questions out loud, and have replies and reports read to you. It works on your phone and on the board, using the speech your phone and PC already have. There is nothing to install and nothing to pay for.

## Speak instead of typing

1. Tap the round **mic** button beside a box where you type: **What do you want?**, the reply box in Goals, or **Tell the agent something**.
2. Allow the microphone the first time your browser asks.
3. Talk. Your words appear in the box as you speak.
4. Tap the red button when you're done. A long pause stops it too.
5. Check the words, fix anything by typing, then tap **Send**.

Nothing sends by itself unless you turn on **Send when I stop talking** (see Talk mode below).

## Answer a question by voice

When an agent asks something, tap **Say your answer** in **The agent asks** and say it, for example "the clinical one is fine". Decurion shows what it heard and marks the matching choice. Tap **Send this answer** or the choice to send it. If what you said doesn't match a choice, tap **Answer with these words** to send it in your own words instead.

In Goals, each of the project lead's questions has its own mic beside **Something else…**. Say your answer and it picks the matching choice, or keeps your words as your own answer. Then tap **Send answers**.

## Hear replies and reports

- Tap **Read aloud** on a project lead's reply, a question, or a finished piece of work's report. Tap **Stop** to stop.
- On your phone's inbox, tap **Read what needs me** to hear everything that's waiting on you, top to bottom.
- To hear new replies from your project lead as they arrive, turn on **Read new replies aloud** in **Settings**, under **Voice**.

## Talk mode

Talk mode is for a back-and-forth with your project lead in Goals. It reads each new reply, then turns the mic on for your answer. Turn it on in **Settings**, under **Voice**.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Read new replies aloud** | Reads new replies while the page is open. |
| **Talk mode** | Reads each reply from your project lead, then listens for yours. |
| **Send when I stop talking** | In Talk mode only, sends your answer after you stop talking. Off means you check the words and tap Send. |
| **Voice** and **Speed** | Pick the voice that reads to you and how fast. |
| **Keep my voice on this device** | In Chrome on a PC, tap **Check this device**, then download the speech pack if offered, so your speech turns into words on the PC itself. |

Voice choices are kept on each phone or PC, so set them on each one.

## What happens to your voice

- Decurion never records or keeps sound. Only the words you send are kept, the same as typing.
- Your phone or PC turns your speech into words. On the phone, Chrome uses Google's speech service for that. On a PC, Chrome can do it on the PC after you download the speech pack; Edge uses Microsoft's service.
- The mic is on only while its button is red. It stops when you tap it, pause, or leave the page.
- Agents never hear you. They get your words as text, as if you'd typed them.
- Never say health or personal details, the same as when typing.

## If it doesn't work

- **No mic button:** your browser can't turn speech into words. Use Chrome on your phone or PC. Firefox doesn't have it.
- **"The microphone is blocked":** open the browser's site settings for Decurion, allow the microphone, then tap the mic again.
- **Nothing reads aloud:** tap the page once first. Browsers only play sound after a tap. Check that your volume is up.
- **On your phone, it can't reach the mic at all:** open Decurion at its Tailscale https address. Browsers only allow the mic on secure addresses.
